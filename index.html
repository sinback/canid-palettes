🐺 canid-palettes

What am I looking at?

Dogs, wolves, coyotes and foxes are dichromats: they have two cone types (peaking near 429 nm and 555 nm) where humans have three. Each result row shows your color on the left and a simulation of how a canid sees it on the right.

Each hex color is turned into the light a screen emits, and that light into how strongly each canid cone responds. The right-hand swatch is the yellowish or bluish color that triggers a canid's cones identically (the sliders pick which yellow and which blue): to a canid the two swatches match, and what you see missing is what a canid cannot tell apart. mode rat is an experimental one-cone model, shown in green.

A hex color does not say which screen it is on, so a typical one is assumed. For the intended preview, turn off Night Shift, f.lux or similar.
